Fundamentals of Fluid Mechanics

A Systematic Study of Essential Concepts

Course Level: Undergraduate
Prerequisites: Calculus, Basic Physics
Duration: 6 Modules

Course Overview

This course provides a comprehensive foundation in fluid mechanics fundamentals, progressing systematically from basic fluid properties to advanced concepts in dimensional analysis. Each module builds upon previous knowledge, ensuring a solid understanding of the physical principles governing fluid behavior.

Learning Objectives

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

  • Understand and apply fundamental fluid properties in engineering calculations
  • Recognize the validity and limitations of the continuum hypothesis
  • Describe fluid motion using kinematic concepts and mathematical tools
  • Apply conservation principles to solve fluid mechanics problems
  • Analyze rotational flow phenomena and vorticity dynamics
  • Utilize dimensional analysis for problem simplification and scaling
